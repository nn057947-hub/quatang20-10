WEBSITE QUA TANG 20/10 - CUC DANG YEN KHOAA
Nguoi gui: Nam Yeu Khoa

CAU TRUC
- index.html: website chinh
- images/: 4 anh ky niem da gan san
- music/nhac-nen.mp3: nhac nen da duoc them, lap lai lien tuc

XEM THU TREN MAY
1. Giai nen file ZIP.
2. Mo index.html bang Chrome, Edge hoac Safari.
3. Bam nut "♫ Bật nhạc" de phat nhac. Trinh duyet dien thoai thuong can bam nut truoc khi phat.
4. Nhac se lap lai tu dau khi het bai.

DANG WEBSITE MIEN PHI
Tai toan bo noi dung trong thu muc nay len mot repository GitHub, giu nguyen cau truc index.html, images/ va music/. Sau do bat GitHub Pages trong Settings > Pages.

LUU Y
- Khi cap nhat website online, phai tai len ca thu muc images va music, khong chi index.html.
- Nhac va anh da nam trong goi ZIP nay.
